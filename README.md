# Bojan Drasko

I am an embedded and ML engineer who verifies and validates AI accelerator SoCs at Axelera AI, working from Lisbon and across Europe.

Accelerators and signal processing caught my interest early. My first venture was a very small startup, Hero Glasses: glasses that turned nearby sounds into light cues for people who cannot hear; the technology was not ready at the time. From there I moved into integrated-circuit design, working on analog and mixed-signal circuits as well as their verification and layout, and did research work with IHP, the Leibniz Institute for High Performance Microelectronics in Frankfurt (Oder), Germany. Today I am at Axelera AI, which designs its own AI inference accelerators in Europe.

My work is design verification and post-silicon validation of multi-chiplet AI accelerator SoCs: the boot and reset sequences that take a chip from power-on to its operating state, orchestrating workloads across the on-chip AI engines, sign-off before production, and then silicon bring-up and validation in the lab.

On GitHub you will mostly find side projects: LLM inference on consumer GPUs, tooling for orchestrating coding agents, and small embedded and desktop utilities.
